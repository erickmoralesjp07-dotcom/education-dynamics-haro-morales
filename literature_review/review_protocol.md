# Structured literature review protocol

**Review title:** Dynamic modeling of international educational indicators: a structured literature review

**Research question:** How have international educational indicators, panel data, dynamic systems, differential equations, and predictive validation been used to study changes in educational access, completion, literacy, and public financing?

**Primary target journal:** Social Indicators Research

**Alternative journals:** Education Sciences, Journal of Educational Change

## 1. Purpose

This review will identify, classify, and critically evaluate literature related to dynamic educational indicators, international panel data, system dynamics, differential equations, educational financing, access, completion, literacy, predictive validation, and cross-country heterogeneity.

## 2. Review design

The review follows a structured and reproducible search, deduplication, screening, extraction, and quality-appraisal process. PRISMA categories are used to document the flow of records, although the review may include methodological and institutional sources in addition to peer-reviewed empirical studies.

## 3. Databases

- Scopus
- Web of Science Core Collection
- ERIC
- ScienceDirect
- SpringerLink
- Google Scholar
- UNESCO Digital Library
- OECD iLibrary

## 4. Publication period

Primary searches cover 2000 to 2026. Earlier foundational works may be included through backward citation when they are necessary for the mathematical or theoretical framework.

## 5. Languages

- English
- Spanish

## 6. Inclusion criteria

- I01: The study analyzes educational indicators, educational systems, or educational change.
- I02: The study uses longitudinal, panel, dynamic, time-series, system-dynamics, or differential-equation methods.
- I03: The study includes access, exclusion, completion, attainment, literacy, education quality, teachers, infrastructure, or educational financing.
- I04: The study reports enough methodological detail to evaluate data, model, sample, and findings.
- I05: Peer-reviewed studies, authoritative institutional reports, methodological books, or official data documentation.
- I06: Publication available in English or Spanish.

## 7. Exclusion criteria

- E01: The study concerns classroom teaching only and does not analyze system-level educational change.
- E02: Opinion pieces, promotional material, or documents without identifiable methods or sources.
- E03: Duplicate publication or duplicate database record.
- E04: The study reports only a cross-sectional description without temporal, comparative, or methodological relevance.
- E05: Full text cannot be obtained after reasonable institutional and author searches.
- E06: The source, sample, indicators, or estimation procedure cannot be determined.

## 8. Screening procedure

Records will first be deduplicated using DOI, title, authors, and publication year. Title and abstract screening will then be conducted. Potentially eligible studies will proceed to full-text review. Each exclusion at full-text stage will receive one primary reason.

## 9. Data extraction

The extraction matrix records bibliographic data, journal quartile, geographic scope, indicators, sample, period, model, validation, baselines, findings, limitations, causal claims, and reproducibility.

## 10. Quality appraisal

Each included quantitative study will be scored on twelve criteria using 0 for No, 1 for Partial or unclear, and 2 for Yes. Scores will support critical interpretation rather than automatic exclusion.

## 11. Thematic synthesis

- SDG4 and educational indicators: Definitions, monitoring frameworks, metadata, and UIS indicators.
- Education and social development: Education as a component of well-being, human development, and equity.
- Educational access and exclusion: Out-of-school rates, participation, enrollment, and access barriers.
- Educational completion and attainment: Completion, survival, transition, graduation, and adult attainment.
- Youth literacy: Literacy definitions, measurement, trends, and ceiling effects.
- Public education financing: Public expenditure, allocation, efficiency, and educational outcomes.
- Dynamic systems in education: Feedback, stocks, flows, system dynamics, and educational complexity.
- Differential equations in social sciences: Continuous-time models, finite differences, and dynamic interpretation.
- International educational panel data: Cross-country panels, temporal coverage, comparability, and dependency.
- Cross-country heterogeneity: Institutional, economic, regional, and policy differences.
- Predictive validation and baselines: Cross-validation, temporal validation, grouped validation, and null models.
- Missing data and unequal indicator coverage: Missingness, irregular observation intervals, and selection effects.
- Negative and null predictive results: Weak prediction, model limits, publication bias, and transparent reporting.
- Ecuador and Latin American education: Regional educational trends, policy context, and national evidence.

## 12. Reporting principles

- No reference will be included without a verifiable title, author, year, and source.
- Journal quartiles will be recorded with their corresponding year and database.
- Weak, null, or negative results will be included when methodologically relevant.
- Correlation and prediction will not be described as causal evidence unless the study design supports it.
- Exact quotations will include page numbers.
- Search dates, filters, exports, and result counts will be preserved.
