# Public repository and reproducibility notice

This repository contains code, environment specifications, analytical outputs, validation diagnostics, and manuscript-preparation materials for the education-dynamics study.

## Authors

- Erick Jair Morales Portilla - ORCID: 0009-0009-2228-4140
- David Sebastian Haro Perugachi - ORCID pending

Both authors are affiliated with the Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Engineering program, Faculty of Engineering in Applied Sciences (FICA), Universidad Tecnica del Norte, Ibarra, Ecuador.

## Data

The original education indicators were obtained from the UNESCO Institute for Statistics. Large original and processed datasets are not redistributed. Scripts and documentation reconstruct the analytical workflow from official source data. Third-party scholarly full texts are excluded from version control.
